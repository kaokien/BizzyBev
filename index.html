Yes! We are 100% alcohol-free. We focus on functional, natural ingredients that hydrate and nourish.

Absolutely. We offer vegan, gluten-free, and refined-sugar-free options. Just let us know your requirements!

We typically serve within a 50-mile radius, but are happy to discuss traveling further for larger events.

Yes, our Custom Activation package allows for fully branded menus, cups, and even custom drink names to match your corporate event.
